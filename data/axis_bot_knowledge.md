# Axis Bank MY ZONE Credit Card - Benefits & Knowledge Base

You are equipped with the following information regarding the Axis Bank MY ZONE Credit Card. Only refer to these offers when the customer explicitly asks about them or when trying to gently persuade them to activate their card.

## Lifestyle & Entertainment Offers
- **Movies (District App / Paytm)**: Buy 1 Get 1 Free on movie tickets. Get up to Rs. 200 discount per month.
- **OTT Subscriptions**: Get a 1-year complimentary SonyLiv Premium subscription after the first transaction.

## Dining & Shopping Offers
- **Swiggy**: Flat Rs. 120 off on food delivery orders above Rs. 500 (Available twice a month).
- **AJIO**: Up to Rs. 1000 off on select styles. Minimum spend requirement is Rs. 2,999.
- **EazyDiner**: Up to 15% discount at 4,000+ partner restaurants (Maximum benefit up to Rs. 500).

## Travel & Rewards
- **Airport Lounge Access**: 4 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per year. (Note: This card does NOT include International Lounge Access).
- **Reward Points (EDGE)**: Earn 4 EDGE Reward points for every Rs. 200 spent. (Exceptions: Spends on fuel, rent, insurance, and utilities do NOT accrue reward points).
- **Fuel Benefit**: 1% fuel surcharge waiver for fuel transactions between Rs. 400 and Rs. 4,000.

## Fees & Charges
- **Joining Fee**: Rs. 500. This is frequently waived based on the first transaction or special activation offers.
- **Annual Fee**: Rs. 500 (beginning the second year). This fee is completely waived off if the customer spends more than Rs. 1.5 Lakhs in the year.
- **Interest Rate**: The applicable interest rate is standard at 3.25% per month. 

*(If a customer asks about generic Bounce Charges, EMIs, or unlisted policies, inform them to contact official Axis Bank support.)*
